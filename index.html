How to finish faster

The quickest wins first. These come from what is already in your app.

Do first · 2 minutes

1. Add your booking link once

Three places in the app are waiting for "[your link]": A3, your 30-day plan and the D1 message. Fill your call-booking link once and the A3 message, Word and PDF downloads all unlock.

Today · 30 minutes

2. A3 alone can finish your week

You have 36 past clients. Sending the ready A3 message to all 36 starts up to 36 real conversations, more than the 20 you still need. Send it personally, a few at a time, not as a broadcast.

Today · 10 minutes

3. Message the 5 who asked

Five warm messages asking "what does your child find hardest about studying?" Every reply is one conversation. Count each one in the Leads Lab before you sleep.

Today · 2 minutes

4. Decide your thank-you (A4)

Pick one thing you can easily give, like an extra session or a bonus card pack. Then tell clients about it once in the A3 message. That finishes A4.

Today · 1 minute

5. Tick what you've already done

In Mission 3, step 1 (Leads Lab Steps 1 and 2) is already done. Ticking it moves your mission forward right away.

Every night · 2 minutes

6. Count before you sleep
